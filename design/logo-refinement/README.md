# Logo refinement

Three concepts developed from the selected V1 balanced fan and A2 fan bucket. Earlier design rounds and application assets are unchanged.

- N1 fan scoop: a new open bucket outline with five positive fan spokes and a yellow center. Combines V1's lightness and A2's construction reference.
- V1 refined: five radial spokes with equal lengths and 30-degree spacing around the yellow center; compact and quiet.
- A2 refined: a filled bucket with five negative fan spokes and a yellow center replacing the yellow cutting edge. Boldest silhouette.

Each includes a transparent SVG mark, monochrome SVG, app-icon SVG, and 192 px app-icon PNG. The comparison shows wordmarks, actual 64/32/16 px icon previews, monochrome marks, and dark-background lockups. All cutouts use vector masks and remain transparent. Preview wordmarks use system sans-serif fonts; marks do not require fonts or external resources.
